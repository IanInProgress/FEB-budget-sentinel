# Privacy Policy

**FEB Purchase Bot** (also "FEB Budget Sentinel") is an internal tool for Formula Electric Berkeley ("FEB", "we") that manages team purchase requests and budget tracking through Slack, Google Sheets, and Google Drive.

_Last updated: October 4, 2026_

## Information we access and store

- **Slack data:** user IDs and display names, purchase request details you submit (item, vendor, amount, justification, links), receipt images you share, and manager approval or rejection decisions and reasons.
- **Google Sheets data:** the team budget spreadsheet, including a log of purchase requests and their outcomes.
- **Google Drive data:** receipt files uploaded by the bot to a designated FEB Drive folder.

## How Google user data is used

The bot uses a Google OAuth authorization (Google Drive scope) granted by an FEB administrator solely to upload and organize receipt files in the team's Drive folder. We do not use Google data for advertising, do not sell it, do not transfer it to third parties, and do not use it to train AI/ML models. Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

## Sharing

Data is visible only to FEB members and managers authorized in the relevant Slack workspace, Google spreadsheet, and Drive folder. We do not share data with other third parties, except the infrastructure providers needed to run the bot (Slack, Google, and our hosting provider).

## Storage and security

OAuth tokens and credentials are stored as protected environment variables or secret files on our hosting platform and are not published. Access is limited to FEB administrators.

## Retention and deletion

Purchase and receipt records are retained for team financial record-keeping. To request deletion of your data, or to revoke the bot's Google access, contact us below, or remove access at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

## Changes

We may update this policy; the date above will change accordingly.
